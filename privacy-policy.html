Privacy Policy

Data Controller: Serkan Çoban (operating under the developer brand Sebmist Studio).
Contact: sebmist.studio@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy governs all mobile applications, games, and digital services ("Services") currently published or to be released in the future by Sebmist Studio.

1. Categories of Data Processed

  • Advertising & Device Identifiers: Applications displaying ads may utilize third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdMob), which may process Advertising IDs (AAID/IDFA), IP addresses, device model, OS version, approximate location inference, ad interaction metrics, and fraud-prevention signals.
  • Analytics & Technical Logs: Standard diagnostic, crash, and performance data (such as via Firebase or store diagnostics) may be collected to maintain stability and resolve technical issues.
  • In-App Purchase Records: Payments are processed exclusively by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not collect or store credit card numbers; we only receive non-financial purchase tokens/entitlement statuses to unlock features (such as ad removal or item packs).
  • Gameplay & Local Data: Game progress, scores, and settings are stored locally on your device or synced via platform cloud services where supported.
  • Support Correspondence: If you contact us via email, your email address and message content are processed solely to respond to your inquiry.

2. Purposes & Legal Bases of Processing

We process data to operate and improve our Services, serve contextual or personalized ads, verify in-app purchases, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations. Processing is based on contractual necessity, legitimate interest, legal compliance, or user consent (via ATT/UMP consent prompts) as required under GDPR, KVKK, and applicable laws.

3. International Data Transfers

Our third-party service providers (Apple, Google AdMob/Firebase) operate global infrastructure. Accordingly, technical and advertising data may be transferred to and processed on servers outside your country of residence in compliance with applicable data transfer safeguards.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Our Services do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction) without verifiable parental consent. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us immediately for deletion.

5. Data Retention, Deletion & User Rights

Data is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes stated or as required by law. Under GDPR, KVKK, and local privacy laws, you have the right to request access, correction, restriction, portability, or deletion of your data by emailing sebmist.studio@gmail.com.
