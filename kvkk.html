Turkish Data Protection Notice (KVKK)

Data Controller: Serkan Çoban (operating under the developer brand Sebmist Studio)
Application Email: sebmist.studio@gmail.com

1. Processed Personal Data Categories

Depending on the application and active SDKs, device/app identifiers, advertising IDs (AAID/IDFA), IP addresses, technical diagnostic logs, store purchase verification tokens, support emails, and local gameplay progress/scores may be processed.

2. Purposes & Legal Bases (KVKK Art. 5)

Data is processed to operate applications, serve advertisements, verify in-app purchases, ensure security, and respond to user support requests, based on contractual necessity, legitimate interest, legal obligations, or explicit consent where required under Article 5 of Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

3. Data Transfer (KVKK Art. 8 & 9)

Data may be shared with advertising networks (Google AdMob), app stores (Apple/Google), technical infrastructure providers, and authorized public bodies strictly limited to the stated purposes and in compliance with cross-border transfer rules under Article 9 of KVKK.

4. Data Subject Rights (KVKK Art. 11)

Under Article 11 of KVKK, you may submit requests regarding access, correction, deletion, or objection to data processing by emailing sebmist.studio@gmail.com with your app name, platform (iOS/Android), and request details.
