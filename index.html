SebMİst Studio - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 22, 2026

SebMİst Studio ("We", "Our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile applications (Block Glass, Sudoku Classic, and others) handle your information.

Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact us at:

Email: sebmiststudio@gmail.com

1. Collected Data & Admob

We use Google AdMob to display ads in our apps. AdMob may collect device identifiers (Advertising ID / IDFA), IP addresses, and general usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and prevent fraud. This data collection is governed by Google's privacy policies.

2. Personal Information

SebMİst Studio does not directly collect, store, or process sensitive personal data such as your name, phone number, or address on its own servers. Authentication (Google Play Games / Apple Game Center) is handled securely by the respective platform providers.

3. Account and Data Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your data:

  • In-App: You can reset or clear your progress anytime via the in-app Settings menu.
  • By Email: Send a deletion request to sebmiststudio@gmail.com with your player ID or username. Requests are processed within 48 hours.
